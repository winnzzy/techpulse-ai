# TechPulse AI Revenue Roadmap

TechPulse AI is being built as an AI/technology media, decision-tool, research and commerce platform. Revenue must follow audience trust: editorial rankings are never sold, sponsorship is disclosed, and commercial integrations must not degrade usefulness or Core Web Vitals.

## Funnel

Search / News / Social / Direct → Article or TechPulse Labs tool → Related guide/comparison → Newsletter or account → Affiliate/product/lead → Repeat audience → Premium/B2B.

## Phase 1 — Audience and intent infrastructure (current)

- High-quality topical article clusters.
- Complete step-by-step Guides.
- TechPulse Labs: ROI Calculator, Local LLM Hardware Checker, AI Tool Finder, Token/API Cost Calculator, AI Stack Builder, Model Comparison Explorer.
- Topic hubs, search, RSS, author/editorial standards, structured data and internal linking.
- Advertise & Partner page with editorial firewall.
- No fake ads, fake sponsors, invented traffic metrics or pay-to-rank recommendations.

## Phase 2 — Measurement and conversion foundation

Activate only after real provider/account IDs exist.

- Canonical events: `tool_open`, `tool_complete`, `article_commercial_click`, `affiliate_click`, `sponsor_click`, `direct_ad_click`, `newsletter_cta_click`, `newsletter_signup`, `lead_cta_click`, `lead_submit`, `product_cta_click`.
- CTA click events and successful conversion events remain separate.
- Search Console + sitemap monitoring.
- Newsletter provider and first-party signup forms.
- Reusable disclosure components for affiliate and sponsored content.
- Commercial-link wrapper with destination, placement, article/tool, campaign and partner metadata.
- Conversion dashboards by landing page, content cluster and tool.
- Consent/CMP implementation where required before relevant tracking/ads.

## Phase 3 — Commerce

- Affiliate programs for suitable AI SaaS, developer tools, hosting/cloud, security, productivity and hardware.
- Comparison pages driven by editorial criteria and documented testing.
- Tool-to-commercial funnels: hardware checker → hardware/cloud guides; API calculator → model/API/hosting comparisons; tool finder → software comparisons; stack builder → infrastructure/developer platforms.
- Direct sponsor inventory for newsletter, categories, tools, research and webinars.
- Sponsored content labeled prominently; sponsorship never determines editorial ranking.

## Phase 4 — Advertising

- Apply for AdSense only when the publication has sufficient original depth and launch-quality trust/contact infrastructure.
- Keep centralized ad inventory inactive until an approved provider and verified slot IDs exist.
- Do not create unexplained blank ad areas before activation.
- Start conservatively; measure revenue per session against engagement and performance.
- Later evaluate higher-value programmatic/direct inventory when traffic supports it.

## Phase 5 — Owned products and recurring revenue

- TechPulse Pro: ad-free experience, deeper comparisons, reports, benchmarks and premium newsletter.
- Digital products: AI workflow templates, prompt/workflow packs, local-AI guides, coding-agent configurations and business playbooks.
- Courses/workshops: AI for Business, AI for Customer Support, Local AI, AI Coding Agents.
- Research/data: TechPulse AI Index, pricing trackers, surveys, benchmarks and paid datasets/reports.
- Job board, startup directory featured listings, webinars/events and community when audience scale justifies them.

## Phase 6 — B2B

- AI implementation and workflow consulting.
- Technical/AI-stack audits and training.
- Qualified vendor/implementation lead generation with explicit consent and disclosure.
- Research sponsorship, data/API licensing and content syndication.

## Guardrails

1. Editorial judgment is independent from commercial relationships.
2. Affiliate relationships and sponsorships are clearly disclosed.
3. Never fabricate testing, benchmarks, prices, reviews, users, sponsors or revenue claims.
4. Time-sensitive product/pricing claims require current authoritative sourcing.
5. Track commercial performance without collecting unnecessary personal data.
6. Do not activate tracking, advertising or newsletter integrations until their real configuration and privacy requirements are in place.
7. Optimize revenue per trusted reader, not maximum ad density.
8. Guides must deliver a reproducible working outcome rather than thin explanatory content.

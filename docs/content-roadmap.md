## Editorial strategy

The launch content should concentrate authority rather than scatter across unrelated technology topics. The first 30 articles are weighted toward high-intent AI tooling, practical tutorials, and evergreen explainers. Each piece should add original testing, screenshots, examples, comparison criteria, or analysis before publication.

## Publishing cadence

Target three substantial articles per week during the initial build-out. Publish one commercial-intent comparison/review, one practical tutorial, and one evergreen explainer each week. Update high-intent comparison articles monthly or when a major product release materially changes the recommendation.

## Monetization order

1. Build useful content and organic traffic first.
2. Add analytics and Search Console after production-domain deployment.
3. Add affiliate links only where there is genuine product intent and disclose them clearly.
4. Apply for AdSense after the site has a meaningful library of original articles, complete policy pages, stable navigation, and a fully launched domain.
5. Add newsletter sponsorships only after the newsletter has a real engaged audience.

## Quality checklist for every article

- Clear reader problem and search intent
- Original examples, screenshots, testing, or analysis
- Author/editorial attribution
- Descriptive title and meta description
- Internal links to related cluster content
- Sources for factual or time-sensitive claims
- Updated date when recommendations materially change
- Affiliate disclosure when applicable
- No filler sections written only to increase word count
- No copied manufacturer descriptions or lightly rewritten release notes
